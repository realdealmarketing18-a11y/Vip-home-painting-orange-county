Orange County Sales Page · Seedance Shotlist

One story, five clips — the page as a video sales letter, in chapters

Not disconnected b-roll per section. One throughline, in the same order the copy already runs — the hero’s promise, then the offer proven, then the fascination bullets — so if every clip on the page played back to back it would read as one continuous sales film, and on the page each chapter simply lives inside the section that makes that argument. The flow map below lays out the whole page; five scenes are written to full prompts so far, the rest are planned but not yet scripted.

Decided: hands/backs-only, no faces in frame — confirmed, to conserve credits. A trained Gallagher character/Soul already exists in Higgsfield if faces are ever wanted for a future piece; noted for later, not used here.

Continuity across all four scenes

Location
A pale oak dining table in a sunlit coastal-contemporary living room, tall window camera-left. A generic staged interior — not a specific real client property, so it does not need to trace back to a verified real-home plate the way an actual client house would.
Cast
An unnamed homeowner couple in their fifties, filmed only from behind/above or as hands-only inserts. No faces visible in any frame, ever. Him: navy blazer, short grey hair at the back of the head. Her: cream blouse, shoulder-length blonde hair.
Props
A silver laptop · an open navy-blue presentation folder with a gold-embossed crowned-brush emblem, holding glossy photo prints · a printed itemized estimate page. No paint cans, no colour chips, no swatch cards, no fan decks anywhere — a colour is never shown as a flat sample on this page, only as a full house or a full number.
Screens & text
Every screen and every printed page is generated as a soft neutral glow or out-of-focus fine print — never asked to render legible imagery or text. The real renders, the real prints, and the real “$4.75/sq ft” line are composited on top afterward, not generated. Video and image models both fail at small legible detail; this sidesteps that failure mode entirely.

Global Style Prefix — prepend verbatim to every prompt

Style: 8K commercial, 16:9, photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine. Lighting: bright natural daylight, crisp real shadows. Color: 60:30:10. Camera: physical cine lens, 180° shutter blur. Realism: natural physics, tactile materials, rule of thirds, 24fps. Skin: anti-plastic, pore-level realism, natural hand texture, NOT glossy, NOT waxy. Continuity: identical environment, wardrobe and props across cuts.
Realism pass, 2026-09-22 — sourced from Higgsfield’s own docs, not generic AI-prompt folklore. The “Skin:” line above is lifted from their own worked example for seedance_2_0 (our target model), verbatim in spirit: “anti-plastic, pore-level realism… NOT glossy… NOT smoothed, NOT waxy.” More load-bearing: their dedicated hands/faces failure-mode article says the #1 fix isn’t a keyword at all — it’s describing a hand’s start position and end position, never the motion between them. An open gesture path is exactly what the model has to invent mid-generation, and mid-invention is where extra fingers appear. Every cut below that involved a hand in motion has been rewritten that way. Camera movement (a push-in, a drift) is a different, safer category and was left alone — the fix is for hand/finger motion specifically, not for the camera.
