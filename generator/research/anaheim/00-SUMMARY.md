## THE ONE THING THAT MUST CHANGE FROM IRVINE

**Anaheim is a different tier and the copy has to know it.**

Anaheim Hills' median sale price is **$1,134,500**. That is *below* Stonegate ($1.82M),
Irvine's entry-level village. The guard-gated enclaves reach $1.7M–$3M and Peralta Hills
passes $6M, but the city page cannot borrow Irvine's estate register.

| Write Anaheim as | Not as |
|---|---|
| Value transparency, craft, the cost of doing it twice | Discretion, concierge protocol, white-glove |
| "The street notices" | "Your designer already knows" |

**Exception: Peralta Hills genuinely earns the estate register.** Write that one page the way
Hidden Canyon Irvine is written.

## THE ANGLE — unchanged, and now stronger

**Nobody here competes on colour confidence either.** M-02 was established across ~50
painters in both cities; nothing in the Anaheim set contradicts it. Every competitor signal
is price, speed, tidiness, professionalism.

M-07 sharpens it: reading 17 reviews of the 53-review painter, **every single one is about
the crew** — clean, on time, fair price, "didn't skimp". **Not one mentions choosing a
colour or regretting it.** The entire vocabulary of this market is execution. Nobody is
selling certainty.

## WHAT IS STILL MISSING — do not write around these, leave the slot out

1. **HOA and management company for every community except Peralta Hills.** In Irvine, one
   company (Keystone Pacific) ran four of six villages — the highest-leverage B2B finding in
   that market. **The equivalent play here is unrun.** Follow `HOA-DISCOVERY-RECIPE.md`, and
   check whether Anaheim publishes an official HOA lookup the way `cityofirvine.gov` does.
   **Without this the HOA page cannot be written honestly.**
2. **Architecture per community.** Only Peralta Hills is described (Mediterranean villas,
   custom). Palettes must fit the real housing stock.
3. **Whether these communities run design review.** Likely for guard-gated ones — *likely is
   not verified*, and inventing an HOA rule is a hard-rule violation.
4. **Project totals.** Still no real job data from Fabian. Pricing stays
   "starts at $4.75/sq ft of paintable surface"; leave totals out.
5. **Keyword volume.** DataForSEO returns 403; no keyword data for this cluster. Not a
   blocker — C-05 showed community SERPs are starved, and that is a stronger buy signal
   than volume.
