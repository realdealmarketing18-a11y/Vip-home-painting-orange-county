# GBP VIDEO VERIFICATION — Fontana → Anaheim

Source: [Google's official requirements](https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705)
scraped 2026-07-27, plus current practitioner guidance.

**Your situation:** service-area business, changing a verified address. The 9 reviews carry
over. Expect re-verification.

---

## ⚠️ BEFORE YOU RECORD — the setting that fails people

Google's own words:

> *"If you don't serve customers at your business location, do not add your business address
> to your Business Profile."*

You're a service-area business. **Set the address hidden and list service areas only.** If the
address is publicly displayed and customers aren't served there, that alone can fail you.

---

## THE THREE THINGS THE VIDEO MUST SHOW

Google requires all three, in **one continuous recording**, **minimum 30 seconds**.

### 1 · Where you operate
Street signs, nearby landmarks, house/building numbers around the Anaheim address.
This ties your pin to the real world.

### 2 · Proof the business exists
Professional tools, equipment, products, workspace, **branded apparel**.

For a painter, film:
- **Graco or Titan airless sprayer** — serial plate, hoses, tips
- Sherwin-Williams Emerald / Duration buckets, labels facing camera
- Ladders, drop cloths, masking gear, extension poles
- **Branded van or vehicle** — magnetic sign or decal with "VIP Home Painting"
- Branded shirts, hats, business cards
- Marketing materials with your name on them

### 3 · Proof you manage it
Access to things only the owner has:
- **Unlock the branded van** on camera
- Open a locked storage area or equipment cage
- **Your CSLB contractor's license** — hold it steady, readable
- A business permit, insurance certificate, or invoice **with the name matching the profile exactly**

---

## THE RULES THAT CAUSE REJECTIONS

| Rule | Why people fail |
|---|---|
| **One continuous take** | Stopping and restarting invalidates it |
| **No other people's faces** | Google's explicit requirement |
| **No talking** | Practitioner consensus: voices and background conversation get flagged |
| **Minimum 30 seconds** | Aim for 60–90 |
| **Name must match exactly** | "VIP Home Painting" on documents, van, everything — not "VIP Premier" |
| **Steady and readable** | Blurry documents fail. Hold each item 3–4 seconds. |

---

## THE SHOT LIST — record in this order

1. **0:00–0:15** — Street sign at the corner. Pan slowly to nearby landmarks and building
   numbers. No talking.
2. **0:15–0:25** — Walk toward the vehicle. Show the branded van/truck exterior with
   "VIP Home Painting" clearly visible.
3. **0:25–0:35** — **Take out keys and unlock the van on camera.** This is your proof of
   management moment. Don't rush it.
4. **0:35–1:00** — Open the doors. Slow pan across the equipment: Graco/Titan sprayer,
   Sherwin-Williams buckets with labels facing out, ladders, drop cloths, masking gear.
5. **1:00–1:20** — Hold up, one at a time, steady and readable:
   - CSLB contractor's license
   - Business permit or insurance certificate
   - A recent invoice showing "VIP Home Painting"
6. **1:20–1:30** — Final shot: branded shirt or business card next to the equipment.

**Total: ~90 seconds. One take. Silent. No faces.**

---

## IF IT FAILS

You get another attempt. Google tells you the reason. Most common fixes:
- Documents weren't readable → hold longer, better light
- Not continuous → re-record in one take
- Name mismatch → make every document say **VIP Home Painting**
- Address visible when it shouldn't be → hide it, service areas only

**Do not change the business name and the address in the same cycle.** Two simultaneous
changes on a profile under review is a suspension pattern.
