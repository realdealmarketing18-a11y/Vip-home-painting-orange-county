## THE REVIEW SCRIPT — this is the highest-value paragraph here

Don't ask for "a review." Ask for a review that **says the right things**.

Google extracts keyword clusters from review text and shows them as the bolded justification
under your pack listing. The top 3 all carry *pricing*, *professional*, *attention to detail*.
**Nobody carries visualization.**

> **What to say when you hand over the finished job:**
>
> *"If you're happy with it, the thing that helps us most is a Google review — and if you can
> mention the neighborhood, what we painted, and that we showed you the color on your house
> first, that's the part people search for."*

That single sentence seeds three tag clusters, one of which no competitor in either city has.

**Make it frictionless:** short link saved in your phone, texted before you leave the
driveway. Same-day asks convert far better than next-week emails.
