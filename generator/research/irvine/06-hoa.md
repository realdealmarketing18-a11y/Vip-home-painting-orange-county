## WHAT THEY ACTUALLY BUY (not house exteriors)

Woodbury Community Association alone operates **several pools, tennis courts, a
state-of-the-art clubhouse, multiple parks**, and employs **four onsite staff**. The
common-area painting scope across a community like that:

- Clubhouse interiors and exteriors
- Pool houses, restroom buildings, cabanas
- Perimeter walls and community monuments
- Guard houses and gate structures
- Park structures, shade shelters, play equipment surrounds
- Mailbox kiosks, trash enclosures, light poles
- Wrought iron fencing and railings

These are **recurring maintenance contracts on a repaint cycle**, not one-time jobs. An
association with 4,000 units has a maintenance budget and a reserve study that already
schedules repainting.

## 🏛️ THE AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE MARCUS SHOULD USE FIRST

**cityofirvine.gov/community-development/irvine-homeowners-associations**

The City of Irvine publishes an official lookup tool mapping parcel and zoning to **HOA name
and management company**. This is a government primary source — better than any listing site
or blog, and it satisfies the contract's "never invent HOA rules" requirement.

**Use this before any other HOA research method, in every city that publishes one.**

## WHY THIS SEGMENT FITS VIP SPECIFICALLY

1. **The visualization service works even better on common areas.** A board deciding a
   clubhouse color has to justify it to hundreds of residents. Showing the rendered result
   before a vote removes the political risk from the decision — that is a stronger pitch to
   a board than it is to a single homeowner.
2. **Boards are the definition of regret-averse.** They spend other people's money under
   scrutiny and get voted out over bad decisions.
3. **Itemized pricing is a requirement, not a differentiator, here.** Associations must
   document competitive bids. Our line-item estimate format matches how they already buy.
4. **No painter in either market markets to HOAs.** Same gap as color visualization —
   competitors chase homeowners exclusively.
