## THE ONE ANGLE EVERYTHING SHOULD LADDER TO

**Nobody in this market competes on color confidence.**

Across ~50 painters in Irvine and Anaheim, **zero** carry a single Google review tag about
visualization, color consultation, or seeing the result before painting. Every competitor tag
is price, speed, tidiness, or professionalism — table stakes.

CertaPro *claims* visualization on their Irvine page. There is no tool on the page.
**We have a working one on every page.** Claiming beats nothing; demonstrating beats claiming.

**The supporting math:** a wrong-color repaint costs the entire project again — $4,000–$7,000
for a typical exterior — and almost nobody who regrets a color ever saw it on their own house
first. That is the emotional core. Lead with it.

## TOP THREE COMPETITOR WEAKNESSES

**1. Nobody publishes real pricing.** CertaPro's Irvine page says *"Less than you might
expect!"* — no numbers at all. Our itemized $4.75/sqft-of-paintable-surface transparency is a
genuine wedge, and cost queries are the highest-intent searches in this industry.

**2. Nobody goes below city level.** CertaPro lists 30 cities and zero neighborhoods.
Universal Coat runs 2 services × 20 cities. Stubbins does service × region. **Not one
competitor has a neighborhood page.** Our six have almost no direct competition.

**3. Their content is stale and thin.** CertaPro's Irvine page: 3 generic FAQs, last modified
March 2025.

**Where they beat us — handle these honestly:** CertaPro advertises a **2-year warranty**
(we say 1) and displays **EPA Lead Certified** (we don't). Both are unresolved with Fabian.
Do not write around them dishonestly; if the warranty stays at 1 year, make the case for why
our prep spec makes it the better deal.

## THINGS THAT CONTRADICT OUR ASSUMPTIONS

**1. VIP has 9 reviews, not 120.** The schema claimed 5.0 from 120 — false, and removed. The
competitive bar is ~55–60. **We are roughly 50 reviews behind the leaders in both markets.**
Never write copy implying review dominance. "5-Star Rated" may also be wrong — a directory
lists VIP at 4.6.

**2. Distance is against us in Irvine.** VIP operates from Anaheim. The Maps pack weighs
proximity, so Irvine pack rankings are structurally hard. **Organic is fully winnable** —
CertaPro ranks #1–2 for Irvine from an Anaheim HQ. Write for organic and AI search; don't
promise map dominance.

**3. The community SERPs are starved, not just uncompetitive.** Baseline for "orchard hills
house painters": #1 is CertaPro's generic *Irvine* page, #3–5 are painters in **Michigan and
Connecticut**, #6 is Bakersfield, #10 is a YouTube video about **Denver**. Google has nothing
relevant. A genuinely local page should rank fast.
