## ⚠️ THE FINDING THAT SHOULD SHAPE ALL COPY

**These six communities are NOT one market. The value spread is 5×.**

| Community | Median / avg home | Guard-gated | Zip | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hidden Canyon** | **$9,150,000** | ✅ | 92618 | 1 — ultra |
| **Altair** | **$4,590,000** | ✅ | 92618 | 1 |
| **Orchard Hills** | **$3,509,391** | ✅ | 92602 | 1 |
| **Stonegate** | $1,819,000 | ❌ | 92620 | 2 |
| Portola Springs | not published | ❌ | 92618 | 2 |
| Woodbury | not published | ❌ | 92620 | 2 |

A $9.15M Hidden Canyon estate and a $1.8M Stonegate home are different customers with
different anxieties. **Do not write them the same way.**

- **Tier 1 (Hidden Canyon, Altair, Orchard Hills)** — guard-gated. Lead with discretion,
  concierge scheduling, gate protocols, and the founder's walkthrough. Price is not the
  objection; getting it wrong in front of neighbors is.
- **Tier 2 (Stonegate, Portola Springs, Woodbury)** — lead with value transparency,
  itemized pricing, and the cost of a repaint done twice.

Hidden Canyon had only **1–2 active listings** at scrape time. It is tiny and exclusive —
volume will be low, but a single project is worth many Stonegate jobs.

## OPEN GAP

**Five of six HOA associations are unverified.** Only Altair Irvine Master Association is
confirmed by name. The contract forbids inventing HOA rules, so community copy must speak
generally ("your community association's design review") until the association sites are
found. This is the highest-value remaining research task — HOA guidance is a genuine
differentiator no competitor writes about.
