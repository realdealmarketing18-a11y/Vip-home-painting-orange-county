## THE ONE IDEA THE WHOLE FUNNEL RUNS ON

Every competitor in Orange County sells the quality of the brushstroke. We sell **certainty
about the color before the brush is loaded.** Across ~50 painters in Irvine and Anaheim,
**zero** carry a review tag about visualization (`OFFER.md`). It is the only uncontested
position we hold, so every step of the funnel leads with it.

And the research sharpened *how* we argue it. The old line — *"a paint chip tells you almost
nothing"* — aims at the lazy buyer who grabbed a chip at the store. **Our buyer is the
opposite of lazy.** The documented case in `07-VOICE.md` did everything the industry
recommends: weeks of research, candidate colors, 2×2 swatches on three exposures, staff at
the paint store, input from friends. The house still came out wrong.

> **The claim is not "shortcuts fail." The claim is "doing it carefully fails too."**
> A small sample cannot predict a large surface. The only thing that predicts your whole
> house is your whole house.

That turns the visualization from a convenience into a **category correction** — and a
category correction is a position, not a feature. Step 1 makes that argument. Step 2 proves
it on a real house. Step 3 offers it on theirs.

### The arithmetic that makes it provable

A 2×2 ft swatch is **4 square feet**. An Orange County exterior is roughly **3,000**. That is
**750 times larger**, in a light the swatch never sat in. No citation needed, nobody can
argue with it, and it is the single strongest asset this funnel has. Use it early and often.

**Never run Step 3 to cold traffic.** She has not yet agreed that her method is broken. An
offer to fix a problem she does not think she has reads as an ad for a painter, and there are
fifty of those.

## STEP 1 — NAME THE PROBLEM

**Rule that governs this step:** the company name is **banned from the opening**. Open with
*her* — her house, her decision, her method. `vip-copywriter-agent` sells the idea first and
the company last. (The page copywriter does the reverse; do not cross the rules.)

### The 10 drafted, and the one that survived

Part F says draft ten and kill nine. Here is that work, done, so it does not get skipped:

| # | Formula | Draft | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F5 | "Why painting swatches on your house is the worst way to choose a color." | ✅ **Survivor.** Attacks the industry's own best practice. Only we can say it. |
| 2 | F5 | "Why paint chips are the worst way to choose a color." | ❌ Kill — aims at the lazy buyer. Any painter could post this. |
| 3 | F6 | "What you should never do before you sign an exterior painting contract." | ⚠️ Hold for Step 3 — it is a trust hook, not a color hook. |
| 4 | F2 | "Nine color mistakes no painter in Orange County will tell you about." | ⚠️ Hold — needs nine real ones written first. Do not run an empty promise. |
| 5 | F8 | "She taped fourteen chips to her stucco. For six weeks." | ❌ Kill — invented person. See **What fails an ad** below. |
| 6 | F3 | "How to know your color is right without repainting to find out." | ✅ Runner-up. Good Step 2 opener. |

| 8 | F1 | "Seven things to know before you repaint in Orange County." | ❌ Kill — fails the kill test. Any competitor's logo fits it. |
| 9 | F4 | "Choose your exterior color in one afternoon, even if you have stalled two years." | ⚠️ Hold — strong, but only true once the 30-minute consult is booked. Step 3. |
| 10 | F6 | "Four square feet cannot tell you what three thousand will look like." | ✅ **Survivor, and the strongest line available.** Arithmetic, not opinion. |

**Run #10 as the hook and #1 as the turn.** That is the whole ad.

### Script — 22 seconds

> **0–3s (hook, no logo, no company name):** "A two-by-two swatch is four square feet. Your
> house is about three thousand."
>
> **3–9s (the turn):** "Which means the method everybody recommends — paint a few samples,
> look at them for a week — is a test that is seven hundred and fifty times too small.
> Colors do not read the same at scale. That is why a white that looked warm on the wall
> leans pink across the whole facade at four in the afternoon."
>
> **9–17s (the correction):** "The only thing that predicts your whole house is your whole
> house. So we render your actual elevation in every scheme you are considering, in your
> light, before anything is scheduled."
>
> **17–22s (name, then ask):** "VIP Home Painting, Orange County. The consultation is
> complimentary and it takes thirty minutes."

**Caption:** lead with the arithmetic again, because most of the feed is muted.

### Rotation — three more openers off the same argument

- **F7 ·** "Your neighbor's white will not read the same on your house. Same paint, different
  light, different answer."
- **F6 ·** "Never judge an exterior color from a sample you can hold in one hand."
- **F5 ·** "Everything about the sample is right except its size."

Each one is a different door into the same room. Rotate creative, never the argument.

## STEP 2 — PROVE THE FIX

The work is already built: the hero reel on **viphomepainting.com**. One real unpainted house,
the same camera, the same dusk light, four schemes sweeping across it, ending on the one that
was chosen — **Coastal Organic Compound**. That is the ad. Film the screen or export the
sweep; do not re-stage it.

**Why this beats any claim:** CertaPro's Irvine page *claims* visualization and has no tool on
the page. Claiming beats nothing. **Demonstrating beats claiming.** Step 2 is the demonstration.

### Script — 35 seconds

> **0–4s:** "This is the same house four times. Only the color changed."
>
> **4–20s (let the sweep run, narrate lightly):** "Riviera Tuxedo. Euro-Industrial. Newport
> Admiral. And the one they chose — Coastal Organic Compound. Every one of these is the
> actual elevation, not a mock-up of a house that looks like it."
>
> **20–28s:** "Eleven schemes. One decision. Thirty minutes. No test patches, no waiting a
> week to see whether you were wrong."
>
> **28–35s:** "The other seven are on the page — link below. VIP Home Painting."

### Alternates
- **F3 ·** "How to know your color is right without repainting to find out." → run the sweep.
- **F8 ·** "One house. Eleven color schemes. Thirty minutes." → counter overlay on the sweep.
- **Carousel ·** one scheme per card, before on the left of every card, chosen scheme last.

**CTA:** to the page, never to the phone. She is not ready to be called yet, and the page
carries the visualizer that does the next step of the selling for us.

## STEP 3 — ASK FOR THE 30 MINUTES

Only to warm audiences. Formula **B5** (avoid the mistake → get the outcome → complimentary
service) and **B2** (how to, in a timeframe, without the negative).

### The offer, stated once, plainly

> A complimentary 30-minute consultation. Our design team renders your actual home in every
> scheme you are considering, and you get an itemized estimate — prep, primer, coating, trim
> and accents priced line by line. VIP exterior work **starts at $4.75 per square foot of
> paintable surface**. Two-year warranty on labor and materials.
> Call **(909) 312-5400** or book on the page.

Three things in that paragraph are things no competitor on page one does: render the actual
house, publish a rate at all, and itemize before signing. Say all three. Never claim the
warranty is longer than anyone else's — CertaPro advertises two years as well.

### Drafted openers

- **B5 ·** "Avoid the repaint that costs the whole job twice. See your colors on your own
  house first — complimentary."
- **B2 ·** "See your home in every color you are considering, in thirty minutes, without
  painting a single test patch."
- **B5 ·** "You are about to make a decision that stays on the house for the next decade.
  Look at it first."
- **Direct ·** "Complimentary Custom Visualization for Orange County homeowners. Thirty
  minutes, your actual elevation, an itemized estimate before you sign."

### The DM / reply script

When someone comments or messages, answer the question she is actually asking — *will this
be right?* — not with a pitch:

> "Happy to show you. Send a photo of the front elevation and roughly which direction it
> faces, and we will render it in whatever colors you are weighing — that part is
> complimentary. If you want the estimate too, it takes about thirty minutes in person."

Never open a DM with the company name. Never send a price before she has seen her house.

## HOW THE THREE STEPS HAND OFF

```
Cold reel (Step 1)          →  watched ≥50%            →  Step 2 retarget
Step 2 reel / carousel      →  clicked to the page     →  visualizer + page does the selling
Page visitor, no booking    →  Step 3 offer ad
Booked                      →  out of all three audiences
```

**Exclude bookers from every audience.** Nothing burns goodwill with this buyer faster than
being sold something she already bought.

## WHAT FAILS AN AD — check before anything goes live

Every one of these is build-enforced on pages (`verify-site.js`) and must hold on ads too,
where nothing checks it automatically. **This list is the check.**

- ❌ **"Free"** → always **"complimentary"**
- ❌ **"AI", "AI-powered", "automated", "machine learning"** → **"our design team"**, **"the
  Custom Visualization Service"**. Never explain the mechanism; sell the certainty.
- ❌ **"Averages $4.75"** → **"starts at $4.75 per square foot of paintable surface"**
- ❌ **Review counts, star ratings, "5-Star Rated", any aggregate rating.** VIP has **9**
  reviews and the rating is unconfirmed. A false one reached a live page once already.
- ❌ **Project counts** ("500 estates") — no verified count exists.
- ❌ **Invented people.** No client stories, no named homeowners, no "she taped fourteen
  chips to her stucco." **The Gallagher family is unverified** (`STORY-SLOTS.md`) — their
  house is on the page as a render subject, and they must not be described as clients in an
  ad until that is confirmed.
- ❌ **The Houzz poster's words as a testimonial.** She is a public forum poster, not a VIP
  client. Use her *vocabulary* — "reads", "leaning", "griege", "swatches", "my lighting",
  "so permanent", "rookie mistake" — in our own sentences. Never in quotation marks, never
  attributed, never implied to be a customer.
- ❌ **$12,000–$15,000.** Marked `[VERIFY]` in `07-VOICE.md` — it is worked from our own rate,
  not from a closed job. **It does not go in an ad until a real Newport or Coast job backs
  it.** Until then the safe version of that argument is the one with no number in it: *the
  second one costs what the first one did.*
- ❌ **Fake scarcity** — spots left, deadlines, countdowns. `BRAND-VOICE.md`: no urgency
  theater.
- ❌ **An address in a city VIP does not operate from.** Base is Anaheim.
- ❌ **Any phone number other than (909) 312-5400.**
- ❌ **"VIP Premier Painting."** It is **VIP Home Painting**, always.

### And the two tests every draft has to pass

> **The kill test —** could a competitor put their logo on this unchanged? If yes, rewrite it.
>
> **The scale test —** does this ad aim at the careless buyer or the careful one? If it only
> lands on someone who grabbed a chip at the store, it is aimed at the wrong person. Our buyer
> did the homework. Tell her the homework was not enough.
