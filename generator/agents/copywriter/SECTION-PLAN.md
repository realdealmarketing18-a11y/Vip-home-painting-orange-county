## PART 3 · THE THREE STRUCTURAL CALLS

### A · The guarantee is on the wrong thing

Section 6 currently guarantees the **paint job** — 2 years, labour and materials. Suby step 2:
*"always sell the thing you do better than your competition."* **CertaPro advertises 2 years
as well.** The warranty is parity, not strength.

**VIP's strength is the visualization** — uncontested across ~50 painters. That is where a
power guarantee can be specific, measurable and unmatched, in the Domino's shape.

**Blocked on test T4.** Step 3 demands the guarantee be *measurable*, and the render turnaround
has never been measured. Until it is, a guarantee on the visualization cannot be written
honestly. **The 2-year warranty stays exactly where it is and says exactly what it says.**

### B · Social proof has to change route, not be deleted

Reviews are unusable — 9, rating unconfirmed, and a false rating has reached a live page twice.
But Suby's §9 offers a second route: **"research statistics, or quotes from credible or
authoritative sources."** VIP has three, all sourced:

- Coastal repaint cycle **7–10 years → 15–20** with elastomeric *(Finding 2)*
- **Solar-reflective pigment keeps darker colours cooler** *(Sherwin-Williams, Finding 7)*
- **LRV** connects colour choice to cooling load *(Finding 6)*

That is a stronger social-proof section than nine reviews would have been, and it is citable —
which reviews are not.

### C · The form placement is a genuine conflict

p187 puts **Collect Information** at the bottom. The form is currently **Step Five of the
visualiser**, mid-page — because the story puts it there: five steps, her house last.

Three options, **not decided**:

1. **Leave it.** The story reason is strong and the section already works.
2. **Repeat it** at the bottom after the offer block. Suby warns against extra *options*, not
   extra *opportunities* to take the same one.
3. **Move it.** Cleanest framework fit, worst story fit — it would end the visualiser on a
   garage door instead of on her own house.
