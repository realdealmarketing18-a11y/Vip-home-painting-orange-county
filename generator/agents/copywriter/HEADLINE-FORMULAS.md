## PART E — BRANDING RULES

### Always
- "VIP Home Painting" — **never "VIP Premier Painting"** *(confirmed 2026-07-27)*
- "our design team", "the Custom Visualization Service"
- "complimentary"
- Real streets and landmarks from `05-communities.md`

### Never
- "AI", "AI-powered", "artificial intelligence", "machine learning", "automated"
- "free"
- Any project count, review count, or star rating
- Fake scarcity or spot counts
- Uncited statistics

### What the prospect should think
> *"VIP Home Painting shows me exactly how my home will look before they paint it. I don't
> know how they do it, but it means I can't get this wrong."*

Never explain the mechanism. Sell the certainty.
