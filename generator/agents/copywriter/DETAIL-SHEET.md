## HOW TO USE IT

**Features tell. Benefits sell.** The left column is what VIP *does*. The right column is
what it *does for her* — and per p158, the chain is pushed past the obvious one. A latex
mattress does not just feel nice; you sleep, you perform, you get the promotion. Same
discipline here.

**Her words, not ours.** Where the benefit column uses *reads*, *permanent*, *swatches*,
*your light*, *rookie mistake* — those are verbatim from `07-VOICE.md`'s glossary. Suby's
market language rule and VIP's voice research agree.

> ⚠️ **Nothing in the right column may exceed what the left column can prove.** Every

## 4 · METHOD — the part that decides whether it lasts

**This is the strongest section on the sheet and the least used on the page.** Every row is a
mechanism with a consequence, which is exactly what Suby's benefit column is for.

| Feature | What it does for her |
|---|---|
| **Back-rolled on stucco**, not spray-only³ | Sprayed mist lands on the **peaks** of the texture and never reaches the valleys. Back-rolling works the wet coat down into the surface before it dries, filling pinholes and bonding it to the wall. **Spray-only sits on the high points and peels** — often within a couple of years, and on a facade she just paid five figures for. |
| **Two coats at full wet-mil thickness, never over-thinned**⁴ | Target wet film is 4–6 mils, drying to roughly 1.5–2.5 mils a coat, so two coats reach the manufacturer's intended 3–5 mil dry film. Thin it to stretch a bucket and **UV reaches the substrate**, bringing premature chalking, cracking and fade. Over-thinning also compromises the coating's chemistry, so the paint she paid a premium for never performs as the premium product. |
| **Stucco crack and patch repair floated to match texture** | The repair disappears. An unfloated patch reads as a smooth scar across a textured wall in raking afternoon light — visible forever, from the street, on the elevation everyone sees. |
| **Wood repair before coating** | Paint does not fix rot; it hides it until it is worse and more expensive. Fixing first means the next repaint is a repaint and not a rebuild. |
| **Pressure washing** | Coating over dust and chalk is coating over a release layer. Washing first is why the film sticks to the house rather than to the dirt on it. |
| **Full masking and containment** | Her cars, windows, roof tiles, plants and her neighbour's driveway come out the way they went in. |
| **Wind-checked spray scheduling** | Nobody works in conditions that put overspray on the neighbour's Tesla. |
| **Raking-light inspection at completion** | Low-angle light is what exposes lap marks, thin spots and misses. Inspecting under it means the flaws are found by VIP, in daylight, before she finds them herself a week later. |
| **Founder's walkthrough at completion** | The person whose name is on the company signs off the work, and she has someone specific to talk to. |
| **Clean site every evening** | She lives in the house while it happens. Her weekend is not a job site. |

³ [Titan Tool: when and why to back-roll](https://www.titantool.com/blog/when-and-why-to-back-roll-after-paint-spraying) · [BLUE Painting](https://bluepainting.com/the-back-rolling-technique-why-exterior-spray-painting-alone-isnt-enough/)
⁴ [Paint & Coatings Resource Center: WFT/DFT](https://www.paintcenter.org/rj/oct04w.php) · [KTA: coating thickness range](https://kta.com/recommended-coating-thickness/)

## WHAT THIS SHEET MAKES POSSIBLE

Suby's claim is that a proper Detail Sheet writes the offer for you. Reading the right column,
three candidates fall out on their own — **none of them decided, all of them for the magic
wand exercise on p160:**

1. **The method rows are the strongest unused material VIP owns.** Back-rolling and full
   wet-mil are the difference between a facade that lasts and one that peels in two years,
   both are now sourced, and neither appears anywhere on the site. They are also
   *demonstrable* — which is what the swipe sections exist for.
2. **The coastal row is the only claim with a number attached to a timeframe** — 7–10 years
   becomes 15–20. That is Enso-shaped, and it has been researched and cited since before this
   session started without ever reaching the page.
3. **The strongest risk reversal is already in the offer and unstated** — she receives the most
   valuable part *before* paying anything. Suby's rationale (Part 1 of the Seven) asks why the
   offer is so generous. That is the answer, and the page has never said it.
