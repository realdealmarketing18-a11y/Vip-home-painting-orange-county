Frequently Asked Questions

Orange County painting — your questions answered.

How much does exterior house painting cost in Orange County CA?+
Exterior house painting in Orange County typically ranges from $4.75 to $5.50 per square foot depending on the city and home size. Newport Beach and Newport Coast estates average $5.50/sqft; Irvine averages $4.75/sqft. Every estimate includes a Custom Visualization Service. Call (909) 312-5400.
Do you paint homes in Newport Beach and Newport Coast?+
Yes. VIP Home Painting provides full exterior and interior painting in Newport Beach, Newport Coast, Corona del Mar, and surrounding luxury communities, using Sherwin-Williams premium paints and backed by a 2-Year Craftsmanship Guarantee.
Do you offer painting services in Coto de Caza and Irvine?+
Absolutely. We serve Coto de Caza, Irvine, Laguna Beach, Mission Viejo, Dana Point, Ladera Ranch, and all Orange County communities — specializing in luxury residential exterior painting for gated and high-value neighborhoods.
How long does it take to paint a house exterior in Orange County?+
Most Orange County residential exterior painting projects are completed in 3 to 5 days, including color consultation, surface preparation, priming, and painting — with minimal disruption to your schedule.
What paint brands do you use for Orange County homes?+
We exclusively use Sherwin-Williams premium exterior lines such as Emerald and Duration, engineered to withstand Southern California's coastal UV and heat. We never use builder-grade paint on luxury homes.
Are you licensed and insured to paint homes in Orange County CA?+
Yes. VIP Home Painting is fully Licensed, Bonded, and Insured in the State of California, carrying general liability and workers' compensation coverage on every Orange County project.
