The Common-Area Specification

Association Painting SpecificationPrepared for Irvine associations
Surface PreparationPressure wash, scrape and sand failing edges, stucco crack and patch repair floated to match existing texture, bonding primer on bare substrate.
Coating SystemSherwin-Williams Emerald and Duration exterior acrylics on bodies and walls; urethane trim enamel on doors, railings and iron. Two coats at full wet-mil.

No. 09
Board & Manager Questions

HOA Painting — Answered

Do you work with Irvine HOA management companies?+
Yes. Most Irvine villages are professionally managed, and we work with managers directly — walking properties, submitting itemized bids, and coordinating schedules and resident notice without requiring board meetings for every step. Managers can request a bid by phone at (909) 312-5400 or by email.
What common areas do you paint for associations?+
Clubhouse interiors and exteriors, pool houses and cabanas, perimeter walls, community monuments, guard houses and gate structures, park shelters, mailbox kiosks, trash enclosures, and wrought iron fencing and railings. If it is association-maintained and it takes paint, it is in scope.
How do you price common-area painting?+
Itemized by structure rather than as a single lump figure, so your board can compare our proposal against others line by line and approve scope selectively if the budget requires phasing. Pricing depends on surface area, substrate condition and access. We measure on site rather than estimating from plans.
Can the board see the colors before approving them?+
That is the part boards tell us matters most. Our design team renders the actual buildings in each candidate scheme, so the board votes on something residents can see rather than a swatch. It removes the political risk from a decision that several hundred people will have an opinion about.
What insurance and documentation do you provide?+
Certificates of insurance naming your association as additional insured, California contractor licensing details, general liability and workers’ compensation coverage, a written work schedule with resident-notice dates per building, and Sherwin-Williams product data sheets for your reserve study records. All supplied with the proposal, not after the award.
Will the work disrupt residents or close amenities?+
We sequence building by building so amenities stay open wherever possible, and agree work hours and resident-notice lead times with management before anyone arrives on site. Crews are briefed on gate and pool-area protocol, landscaping and hardscape are protected, and the site is left clean every evening.
