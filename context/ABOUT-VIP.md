$2.74–$4.89, so this is the **premium end** — correct for the positioning.

> ✅ "VIP exterior work **starts at** $4.75 per square foot of paintable surface."
> ❌ "Irvine **averages** $4.75." ← contradicted by public data

Every estimate is **itemized line by line** — prep, primer, coating, trim, accents priced
separately. No lump sums. Competitors publish no pricing at all; CertaPro's Irvine page says
"Less than you might expect!"

**Paintable surface ≠ home square footage.** For two-story stucco, paintable runs roughly
0.85–1.1× home sqft. Never price against interior living area.
