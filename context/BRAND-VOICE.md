# BRAND VOICE — how VIP sounds

Rules with examples. If a sentence could appear on any painter's website, rewrite it.

---

## THE VOICE IN THREE WORDS

**Quiet authority.** Editorial, not salesy. We are the expensive option and comfortable
saying so — no exclamation marks, no urgency theater, no shouting.

Think a well-edited home magazine, not a contractor flyer.

---

## THE FOUR RULES

### 1. Specific beats superlative

Generic luxury language is invisible to this buyer. Detail is the proof.

> ❌ "We deliver premium quality and exceptional craftsmanship for discerning homeowners."
> ✅ "Smooth-trowel stucco shows every patch and pinhole, so preparation *is* the finish:
> skim repairs floated to match, elastomeric patching, full prime, then airless spray."

> ❌ "We serve the beautiful Orchard Hills community."
> ✅ "Orchard Hills elevations are judged against the hillside — a shade that looks perfect
> on a swatch can read chalky on your south-facing wall by two in the afternoon."

### 2. Name the fear, then remove it

Our buyer is regret-averse. Acknowledge the risk plainly before offering the fix. Never
pretend the decision is easy.

> ✅ "Three approved whites look nearly identical in your hand and completely different
> across a facade in afternoon sun."

### 3. Never oversell what we haven't earned

We have 9 reviews. Competitors have 55+. Write around the gap honestly — lead with the
visualization, not with social proof we don't have.

> ❌ "Trusted by hundreds of Orange County families"
> ❌ "5-Star Rated" *(rating unconfirmed)*
> ✅ "See your home in every color before a single brushstroke."

### 4. Write to the tier

| Tier | Register |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** — Hidden Canyon $9.15M, Altair $4.59M, Orchard Hills $3.51M | Discretion. Gate protocol, concierge scheduling, household coordination. They already employ a designer. Price is not the objection. |
| **Tier 2** — Stonegate $1.82M, Portola Springs, Woodbury | Value certainty. Itemization, doing it once correctly, the cost of doing it twice. |
| **HOA** | Governance. Documentation, compliance, removing risk from a vote. |

## BANNED — gate-enforced

| Never | Always |
|---|---|
| "free" | **"complimentary"** |
| "AI", "artificial intelligence", "AI-powered" | **"our design team"**, **"Custom Visualization Service"** |
| "Irvine averages $4.75/sqft" | "VIP exterior work **starts at** $4.75 per square foot of paintable surface" |
| `aggregateRating`, "5-Star Rated", review counts | *(omit entirely until verified)* |
| Any phone but (909) 312-5400 in customer copy | **(909) 312-5400** |
| HOA rules not sourced in the brief | "your community association's design review" |

---

## ALSO AVOID

- **Exclamation marks.** One per page, maximum. Preferably zero.
- **Fake scarcity.** "Only 3 spots left!" destroys the positioning.
- **Hype adjectives** — amazing, incredible, stunning, jaw-dropping. Let the specifics carry it.
- **Hedging** — "we strive to," "we aim to." State what we do.
- **Second-person pileups** — "your home, your family, your vision, your dream."
- **Em-dash overuse.** Vary the punctuation.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY & FORMATTING CONVENTIONS

- **Headlines:** Fraunces serif. The accent phrase wraps in `<span class="gold">` — italic gold.
- **Eyebrows:** all-caps Inter, wide letterspacing, gold, ruled either side.
- **Body:** Inter, generous line height. Short paragraphs.
- **Section serials** — *No. 01*, *No. 02* — italic serif. An editorial device, keep it.
- **Numbers:** spell out under ten in prose; use figures for prices, square footage, warranty
  terms, and SW color codes.
- **Sherwin-Williams colors:** always `Name SW 0000` — "Alabaster SW 7008", never "SW Alabaster".

**Colors:** Navy `#1A1F4E` · Gold `#C9A961` · Cream `#F5EFE2` · Orange `#E8833A`

## THE FOUNDER'S VOICE

Fabian appears in a byline on every page. First person, plain, unadorned — the one place the
editorial distance drops.

> *"Every estate deserves to be seen before a single brushstroke. That's my promise to every
> Orange County homeowner we serve."*

Keep it short. Keep it a promise, not a pitch.

Read any sentence and ask: **could this appear on CertaPro's site?**

If yes, it's not ours. Rewrite it with something only someone who has stood on that street,
in that light, in front of that architecture could have written.
