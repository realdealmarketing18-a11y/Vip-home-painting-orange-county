## WHY IT WORKS: THE MATH OF REGRET

A wrong-color repaint costs **the entire project again** — $4,000–$7,000 for a typical
exterior, far more on an estate. And almost nobody who regrets their color ever looked at it
on their own facade first; they chose from a two-inch swatch under showroom light.

The offer eliminates the single most expensive mistake in the category, before a gallon is
tinted. That is not a discount or a bonus. **It is risk removal**, and risk is what our buyer
is actually paying to avoid.

## HOW IT REFRAMES BY BUYER

### Homeowner
*"You're about to spend five figures on a color you've only seen on a two-inch card. See it
on your house first."*
Emotional register: relief. The decision stops being a gamble.

### HOA board / property manager
*"Your board can show residents the finished clubhouse before the vote."*
Emotional register: **political cover.** A board spends other people's money under scrutiny
and gets voted out over bad calls. Showing the render first removes the risk from the vote.

**This is a stronger pitch to a board than to a homeowner** — and no competitor makes it.

## WHAT WE DO NOT LEAD WITH

- **Price or discounts.** Our buyer is regret-averse, not price-sensitive. Leading with price
  attracts the customer we don't want.
- **Reviews or ratings.** VIP has **9** and the rating is unconfirmed. Competitors have 55+.
  Never imply review dominance. Never publish `aggregateRating`.
- **Years in business / crew size.** Unverified.
- **The warranty as a headline.** CertaPro advertises 2 years against our 1. Don't invite the
  comparison in a headline; carry it in the proof stack.
